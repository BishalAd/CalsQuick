# Why Merge PDFs?

Merging PDFs is a common task for professionals, students, and anyone who deals with digital documents. Whether you’re combining report chapters, assembling a portfolio, or collating scanned pages, a reliable PDF merger saves time and ensures consistency.

# How an Offline PDF Merger Works

Unlike online services that require you to upload files to a third‑party server, an **offline PDF merger** runs entirely in your browser. It uses client‑side libraries (such as PDF.js) to parse and combine PDF documents without ever sending your data to the network. This means:

- **Complete privacy**: Your files never leave your device.
- **No file‑size limits imposed by a server**: Only your device’s memory constrains the size.
- **No waiting for uploads or downloads**: Everything happens instantly.

# Best Practices for PDF Merging

1. **Check file order**: Ensure the files are in the correct sequence before merging.
2. **Optimize file size**: Large PDFs with high‑resolution images can be compressed beforehand if needed.
3. **Password protection**: Remove or provide passwords for encrypted PDFs, as most mergers cannot process locked files.
4. **Verify the output**: Open the merged PDF to confirm all pages appear correctly and nothing is missing.

# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is there a file size limit?**  
A: The merger runs entirely in your browser, so the limit depends on your device’s memory and browser constraints. Typically, files up to 100 MB each work fine.

**Q: Are my files uploaded to a server?**  
A: No. All processing happens locally in your browser. Your PDFs never leave your device, ensuring complete privacy.

**Q: Can I rearrange the order of PDFs before merging?**  
A: Yes, you can drag and drop the files in the list to set the desired order before merging.

**Q: What happens if a PDF is password‑protected?**  
A: Password‑encrypted PDFs cannot be processed unless you provide the password. This tool does not bypass encryption.
