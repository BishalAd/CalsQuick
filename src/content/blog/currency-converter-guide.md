## How Exchange Rates Work

The foreign exchange market trades $7.5 trillion daily, making it the largest financial market in the world. Exchange rates are driven by interest rates, inflation, trade balances, and geopolitical events. The mid-market rate — the midpoint between buy and sell prices — is the fairest reference. Banks and exchange services add their own margin (1-7%) on top.

## Avoiding Hidden Currency Exchange Fees

Banks rarely show you their real markup. A $1,000 transfer at the interbank EUR/USD rate of 1.09 gives you €1,090. But at a bank's "special" rate of 1.04, you get only €1,040 — a $50 loss. Airport exchanges are the worst, charging 5-15% markups. Always compare the rate you are offered against the mid-market rate shown by our converter before accepting.

## Smart Travel Money Tips

Never accept dynamic currency conversion when paying abroad — always choose the local currency. Use ATMs at your destination rather than airport exchange counters. Get a credit card with zero foreign transaction fees. For large transfers, use online services like Wise that offer near-interbank rates.
