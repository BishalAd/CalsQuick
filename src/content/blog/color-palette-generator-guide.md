# What Is a Color Palette?

A color palette is a set of colors chosen to work well together in a design. Whether you’re designing a website, a logo, a presentation, or a piece of art, a harmonious palette ensures visual consistency and appeal.

# Why Use a Color Palette Generator?

Manually picking colors that complement each other can be challenging, especially when you need multiple shades that share a common theme. A **color palette generator** applies color theory rules (complementary, analogous, triadic, etc.) to automatically create harmonious schemes from a single base hue.

# How an Offline Color Palette Generator Works

An **offline generator** runs entirely in your browser. It converts your base color to HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness), applies the selected harmony rule by adjusting hue (and sometimes saturation/lightness), and then converts the results back to HEX for display. Because everything happens locally:

- **No data leaves your device**: Your base color and generated palette stay in your browser.
- **Instant results**: No round‑trip to a server.
- **Privacy by design**: Ideal for proprietary brand colors or sensitive design projects.

# Best Practices for Using Color Palettes

1. **Start with a purpose**: Choose a base color that reflects the mood or brand you want to convey.
2. **Test for accessibility**: Ensure sufficient contrast between text and background colors (WCAG AA/AAA). Use tools like WebAIM Contrast Checker.
3. **Use the 60‑30‑10 rule**: In interior or UI design, allocate ~60% dominant color, ~30% secondary, and ~10% accent.
4. **Consider color blindness**: Simulate deuteranopia/protanopia to ensure your palette remains distinguishable for all users.
5. **Document your palette**: Save the HEX, RGB, and HSL values for consistency across media and team members.
